Design Guardrails

Mol's (2015) Three Warnings

More transparency is not automatically fairer. Our rating methodology explicitly prevents these three traps:

1. Can Strengthen the Powerful

Expensive audits and data reporting are easier for large plantations than small Global South farmers. Our fix: Burden of proof sits on the roaster, and missing data receives a provisional 40 pts instead of 0.

2. Can Drown Shoppers ("Data Smog")

230 EU labels and raw metrics like "3.2 kg CO₂e/kg" overwhelm buyers. Our fix: One Nutri-Score-style A–E grade at a glance, with sub-scores behind a QR scan.

3. Requires "Checking the Checkers"

Self-declared brand schemes cannot be trusted equally to independent audits. Our fix: KPI 6 & Confidence Mark weight verified third-party evidence at 2× self-declared claims.

Fairness for Smallholders

Why "Missing Data = 0" Would Fail Small Brazilian Farms

Small family producers in Brazil often lack the administrative staff or auditing budgets to generate formal ESG spreadsheets. If missing data automatically scored 0, the rating would reward wealthy agribusinesses and punish smallholders.

Naive Rule (Rejected): Missing = 0 pts

Penalizes small Brazilian co-ops that cannot afford costly private audits, distorting the shelf score.

Group 3 Rule (Adopted): Provisional 40 pts + Low Confidence

Missing KPIs receive a neutral provisional score of 40/100, visibly flagged with a Low/Medium Confidence badge—placing the burden of proof on the European roaster.

Note: Unpaid family labor accounts for up to ~10% of the retail price in conventional smallholder coffee chains (Coffee Barometer 2026).

KPI 5 Design Insight • Making Carbon Comprehensible

Why We Measure "kg CO₂e per Cup" Instead of "3.2 kg CO₂e per kg"

Shoppers struggle to interpret raw agricultural footprint numbers like "3.2 kg CO₂-equivalent per kilogram"—and per-kg figures hide the massive packaging and brewing energy gap between filter coffee and single-serve aluminium/plastic capsules. Measuring per cup lets consumers compare brewing systems directly.
